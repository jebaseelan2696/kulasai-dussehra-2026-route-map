2. TUT regular route: sequence (SDPO description)

  1. Muthiahpuram checkpost (KMZ: Muthiahpuram Police Station) to confirm
  2. Mukta I roundabout (KMZ: Mukkani Roundabout) to confirm
  3. Adaikkalapuram Jn.
  4. Shanmugapuram
  5. Veerapandiyapattinam
  6. TDR bus stand (KMZ: Tiruchendur Bus Stand)
  7. TDR Iron Arch (KMZ: Tiruchendur Iron Arch)
  8. Mutharamman Jn.
  9. Muruganmadam Jn. (KMZ: Murugamadam Jn.)
  10. Paramankurichi Jn.
  11. Chidambara Street Jn.
  12. Russian Jn. (KMZ: RSUS Jn.) to confirm
  13. Udankudi Bus Stand Jn.
  14. Villikudiyiruppu Jn.
  15. JK Furniture Jn.
  16. Y Jn. (KMZ: Kulasai – Tharuvaikulam – Udankudi 3-way Y Jn.) to confirm
  17. Parking: P-5, then P-6, P-7, P-8, P-9, P-11, P-12 (see section 4)

Destination: C24X+RFP (KMZ). The KMZ line passes these points in the same order.
